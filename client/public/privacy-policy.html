Privacy Policy for Sales CRM

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sales CRM mobile application and web services ("Application", "Service") developed for internal sales management, customer relationships, field worker cash collections, and quality assurance.

1. Information We Collect

  • User Account Details: Name, work email address, employee ID, and contact number provided during login or admin registration.
  • Customer & Lead Management Data: Customer contact details, deal stages, communication logs, and customer interaction records.
  • Audio Recordings & Voice Notes: Business call recordings, voice notes, and customer interaction audio explicitly recorded and uploaded by authorized sales representatives for quality assurance and training.
  • Cash Collection & Daily Reports: Daily collection figures, worker/salesperson names, transaction timestamps, and daily work summaries.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

The collected information is used strictly to:

  • Facilitate customer relationship tracking and business communications.
  • Maintain accurate daily cash collection ledgers and worker accountability.
  • Enable authorized managers/admins to review call recordings for service quality.
  • Generate performance metrics, daily work logs, and backup reports.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement enterprise-grade security measures to safeguard your information:

  • Encrypted Transport: All API communication is strictly encrypted via TLS/HTTPS (cleartext traffic is blocked).
  • Secure Cloud Database: Audio files and transaction records are stored in MongoDB Atlas GridFS cloud storage with role-based access control.
  • Access Limitation: Only authorized organizational personnel (Admin & assigned Employees) can view respective data.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with any third-party advertisers. Data is solely stored on enterprise cloud databases for business operational use.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

Users and administrators can request data modifications or complete deletion of their account records, call recordings, or transaction logs directly within the app or by contacting the system administrator.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact your Organization System Administrator or support team.
